FAQs

Common Inquiries

Find quick answers about roles, workflow tracking, and platform permissions.

Is there a cost to register?

Registration is completely free for both creators and brands. Creators can search campaign briefs, and brands can write draft specs and review applications free of charge.

How do milestones protect work?

When a brand approves a creator application, deliverables are structured into sequential milestones. Creators submit links or draft files for validation. The brand signs off on each milestone before the project transitions to the next phase.

Can I change my account type later?

Accounts are bound to either a "Creator" or "Brand" role during sign-up to ensure interface optimization. If you manage both creator channels and a brand, we suggest registering two separate profiles.
